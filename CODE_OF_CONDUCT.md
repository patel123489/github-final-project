# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive environment for everyone who participates in this project. We expect all contributors and participants to communicate professionally and treat each other with kindness and respect.

## Our Standards

Examples of positive behavior include:

- Being respectful and considerate of others.
- Welcoming different opinions and experiences.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its community.
- Communicating politely and professionally.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, offensive comments, threats, and other conduct that creates an unsafe or unwelcoming environment.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing the standards of this Code of Conduct. They may take appropriate action in response to behavior that violates these standards.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project-related activities.

## Enforcement

Violations may be reported to the project maintainers. Reports will be reviewed fairly and confidentially where appropriate. Maintainers may take corrective action, including warnings, removal of inappropriate content, or restriction of participation.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Project maintainers will consider the circumstances and seriousness of each violation. They may respond with:

1. A reminder of the Code of Conduct.
2. A warning regarding inappropriate behavior.
3. Temporary restriction from project activities.
4. Permanent removal from project participation when necessary.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct was created for the project and is inspired by common open-source community standards.
